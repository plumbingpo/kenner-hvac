Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Lafayette

Most mid-season breakdowns start with skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? That number tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that point and is also showing higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A weak capacitor costs little to replace, yet the same part, neglected through a full season, can ruin the compressor and turn a simple fix into a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast as soon as something sounds or feels wrong in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Lafayette

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, a musty smell at startup. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.
