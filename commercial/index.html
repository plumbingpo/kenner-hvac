Food Service Comfort

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms need to stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units must replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Kenner HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, proper tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts at the door and the heat right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Kenner HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

The paperwork matters as much as the repair. We keep a documentation binder with detailed service logs and temperature and filter records, so you can demonstrate due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms, is put on a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Kenner

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly, throughout Kenner and the surrounding area.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, a faulty ignition system or gas valve, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your facility files. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.

Preventive Care

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

A scheduled maintenance program from Kenner HVAC gets built around your property's specific equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. Visits include inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes at the right MERV rating, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep ahead of peak demand. It's the most cost-effective way to manage commercial HVAC equipment.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Give us a call to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
