Where do most HVAC installations go wrong? Usually before the first wrench turns, when someone matches the new tonnage to the old nameplate and skips the math. At Kenner HVAC, the math comes first. We build a load calculation for your Kenner, LA home from its square footage, insulation levels, windows, orientation, and air leakage, because sizing errors in either direction cost comfort and money every day the system runs. From there the job becomes three connected pieces of work: equipment matched to the load, ductwork able to deliver what that equipment produces, and a commissioning process that proves the result with real readings instead of on paper.

Equipment selection follows the load, and matching matters more than most homeowners are told. The indoor coil, outdoor unit, and blower are rated as matched combinations, and mismatched pairings give up the SEER2 and AFUE numbers on the sticker. We walk you through the real trade-offs between single-stage, two-stage, and variable-speed equipment, blower options, and pairings with a furnace, air handler, or heat pump, in plain language and without pressure. Then the ductwork gets the attention it almost never receives: our experienced technicians measure static pressure, hunt down leaky and undersized runs, and correct the chokepoints while the system is open, because the best equipment loses to bad ducts every time.

Installation day runs on discipline: line sets pressure-tested and evacuated to a micron-gauge-verified vacuum, charge weighed in and confirmed by superheat and subcooling, gas connections leak-tested, venting run to manufacturer spec, condensate trapped and protected by a float switch, and the thermostat configured for the equipment's actual staging. We finish with full commissioning, recording temperature split, temperature rise, amp draws, and static pressure, then walk you through everything before we haul the old equipment away. Why Professional HVAC Installation Matters

Installation decides how the biggest machine in your Kenner home will behave for the next fifteen years, and the costly errors are hard to spot at handoff. An unverified vacuum leaves moisture to breed acid inside the refrigerant circuit. A charge set by guesswork gives away efficiency month after month. A mismatched coil and condenser can never deliver the rated SEER2. Restrictive ducts force the blower to fight excess static pressure for its entire life. Add federal refrigerant rules and the safety stakes of gas piping and venting, and this is clearly not a job for guesswork.

Our experienced technicians treat installation as a chain of proofs, not a list of tasks. The load calculation proves the size. Matched equipment makes the rating reachable. A pressure test and a micron-gauge vacuum prove the circuit is tight and dry, while a weighed charge checked against superheat and subcooling proves the refrigerant side. On the heating side, gas lines are leak-tested, draft is verified, and blower speed is set to hold the rated temperature rise. Finally, commissioning puts the numbers on paper, creating the baseline every future visit is measured against. That documentation is standard on every system we install in Kenner.

How to Prevent HVAC Installation Problems

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Call about new noises or slipping performance early, when fixes are simple
  • Keep a spring and fall maintenance visit on the calendar every year
  • Avoid closing registers, which upsets the airflow balance set at startup
  • Start a regular filter schedule right away, since steady airflow protects the coil and the heat exchanger
  • Rinse the outdoor coil and keep plants trimmed back so the condenser can breathe
  • Flush the drain line before summer to keep algae from clogging it
  • Hold onto your commissioning readings; they make every future diagnosis faster and cheaper
